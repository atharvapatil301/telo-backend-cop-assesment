# FAQ

## Can I choose either task?

Yes. Please choose one task only.

## Can I use a different stack?

You can, but Python + FastAPI + PostgreSQL is preferred because it aligns with the role. If you choose something different, explain why.

## Do I need to deploy it?

No. Local Docker-based setup is enough. Deployment notes are a bonus.

## Do I need authentication?

Not required unless you believe it materially improves your design. If you include it, keep it simple.

## Can I use OpenAI, Claude, LangChain, or LlamaIndex?

Yes. You may use them where relevant. Make sure your design remains explainable and grounded.

## How polished should the UI be?

This is a backend-focused assessment. A UI is not required unless you want to add a minimal one. We will primarily evaluate backend quality.

## What if I cannot complete every idea I wanted?

That is fine. Document what you prioritized, what you left out, and what you would do next.

## What will happen after submission?

Selected candidates will move to a technical discussion or interview round where they may be asked to walk through their submission and explain key decisions.
